## Description

```
StickerVox turns a short clip from your own videos into an animated sticker with sound and sends it to the chat you choose, all from WhatsApp Web in Microsoft Edge.

How it works
1. Open WhatsApp Web and click the StickerVox icon. A panel opens on the right.
2. Pick a video from your computer (MP4, WebM or MOV) or drop it on the panel.
3. Trim the range, frame it, add text or emoji, and pick a shape.
4. Listen to it, choose a chat or group, and send it.

Features
• Trim up to 15 seconds on a thumbnail strip, long videos included.
• Free framing: drag and zoom the picture to choose what shows.
• Text and emoji placed directly on the sticker, with styles (meme, bubble, label, shadow), colors, and the exact moment each one appears.
• Square, rounded or circle shape, with an optional white border.
• Smoothness estimate: before building, it tells you how many frames per second the sticker will get, or how many seconds fit when the range is too heavy.
• Peak-normalized audio with short fades (can be turned off).
• Undo and redo.
• Preview with sound before sending.

Good to know
• The interface is in Spanish.
• Up to 15 seconds per sticker and under 1 MB. With heavy motion, StickerVox lowers the frame rate and quality to fit; if it still does not fit, it asks for a shorter range.
• Recipients need a WhatsApp version that supports sound stickers; otherwise they see the animated sticker without sound.
• Works only on WhatsApp Web (https://web.whatsapp.com) on a computer, not in the desktop app or on phones.

Privacy
Everything is processed in your browser. StickerVox has no servers, no analytics, stores nothing, and makes no network requests of its own. The only thing that leaves your computer is the sticker you choose to send, and WhatsApp Web itself sends it. Full policy: https://github.com/xebastian153/stickervox/blob/main/PRIVACY.md

Disclaimer
StickerVox is an independent, unofficial extension. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by WhatsApp or Meta. It depends on WhatsApp Web and on internal parts that WhatsApp may change at any time; if sending stops working one day, an update will be needed.

Open source (MIT license): https://github.com/xebastian153/stickervox
```
